How the radar scores

One function scores every protocol. No name is ever special-cased.

What counts as pre-token

A protocol is ranked only when its snapshot record carries no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades: its category is not Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX; its name carries no whole word wrapped, staked or staking; it does not begin with an exchange's name; and its name ends in no version marker.

What the numbers do not say

An implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. A protocol "records no token" in the snapshot; that is not proof it has none.

Farm plans

Each plan is derived from the protocol's own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age — and states what each step costs and how long it takes. Never pay to claim: a real claim link comes from the protocol's own page.
